## Four activity answer notes

1. Invite experience without ranking people. Two shares maximum.
2. A/B is not a beauty contest. Ask what evidence would change their vote. A is deliberately constructed; B's privacy cannot be proved from a screenshot.
3. Scripture text is public; a person's saved selection and reflection are private. An anonymous error log is not automatically appropriate for public release. Ask: “Should I be able to read Sarah's private reflection?” No—then show ownership in SQL.
4. Say whether a scenario is ready to release, not merely ready for a preview. Tests passing does not excuse a demonstrated privacy leak.

## Hands-on handoff / slides 18–20

Use START-HERE.md or /workshop. Give five quiet minutes to describe person, problem and smallest version. Circulate with: “What did you expect? What happened? What is the next smallest step?” Protect 45–50 minutes of building. Take two or three voluntary shares; finish with reflection and prayer from the existing guide.
