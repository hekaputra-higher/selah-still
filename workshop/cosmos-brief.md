# Cosmos visual brief

Search: quiet editorial design; warm paper; natural window light; monastic interiors; Bible typography; book margins; meditative spaces; muted sage; ink on cream; Japanese minimalism; slow living; library reading rooms; botanical shadows; contemplative architecture.

Collect 10–15 references, then show only three. Look for generous margins, readable serif typography, restrained colour, few controls, natural texture and calm hierarchy. Do not use copyrighted images in the product without permission.

Optional 30-second audience observation, folded into the design explanation rather than a fifth formal activity: “What do these references have in common?” Take one answer. Translate it into a rule: “You noticed breathing room; our Scripture column will be narrow with generous margins.”

Prepare the collection in your own Cosmos account. This package does not claim a collection was created there.
