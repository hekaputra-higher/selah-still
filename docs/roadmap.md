# Now and later

Now: one passage and chapter, D.W.E.L.L., Auth, save/retrieve private reflections.
Next: editing/deleting reflections, account recovery, more curated passages and usability feedback.
Later: a person describes what they feel or face; the app suggests Scripture with context and background. Validate relevance, privacy and theological framing before building. Suggestions must not claim to be God's direct personal speech.
